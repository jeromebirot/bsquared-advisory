About Us

Independent technology judgment
for complex, high-stakes decisions.

B² Advisory works with executives, boards, and investors to de-risk large-scale technology decisions, guide transformation, and ensure long-term strategic alignment in complex environments — and directly with CIOs, CTOs, mobile operators, network suppliers, and systems integrators who need rigorous, independent scrutiny of their technology strategy and delivery.

Large-scale technology transformations — cloud migrations, OSS/BSS carve-outs, 5G deployments, digital separations — fail more often for governance reasons than technical ones. Boards that rely solely on management reporting, without an independent capability to interrogate architecture decisions, challenge vendor assurances, or assess programme risk, are poorly positioned to intervene when programmes begin to diverge from plan.

B² Advisory is designed to close that gap — providing the continuity that a non-executive director engaging only at board level cannot replicate, and the independence that management cannot provide.

How We Work

Structured for continuity.
Designed for independence.

Advisory engagements are structured around a focused immersion phase — establishing the depth of programme understanding required to provide effective board-level advice from the outset — followed by ongoing oversight continuity, typically one day per week. Each mandate is tailored to the board's specific governance needs, programme complexity, and risk profile.

Board Advisory Mandate
Ongoing independent oversight of a technology programme on behalf of the board or a board committee. Includes SteerCo participation, pre-board briefing notes, and on-call availability between meetings.
Programme Immersion & Assessment
A focused, time-bounded deep-dive into a programme's architecture, governance, risk posture, and vendor landscape — concluding with a confidential board briefing note and recommendations.
NomCo & Investor Advisory
Technology due diligence support for nomination committees assessing CTO or CIO appointments, and for investors evaluating the technology risk profile of infrastructure assets or platform companies.
